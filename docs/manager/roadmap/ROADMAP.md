## Identity & Access — Mission M1, 2026-09-04

Strategy and full rationale: `docs/memory/decisions.md`, 2026-09-04 "Identity model: three human tiers, one session, metered AI". That entry is also the `01-cto` sign-off #30 was waiting for.

**Mission**: replace mocked authentication with a real identity model over three human tiers — visitor, reader (`USER`), staff (`REDATOR`…`SUPER_ADMIN`) — coexisting with the existing machine tier (`/api/agent/*`), make `/profile` truthful, close the access-control defects, and cap AI spend per actor.

**Decisions and interpretations recorded at mission creation:**
- `/missao` as written targets a `tasks/_index.md` registry, a `pnpm task:id` script, and nine agent names (`architect`, `backend`, `frontend-terminal`, `product-finance`, …) that do not exist in this repository — `.claude/commands/missao.md` is untracked and carries another project's conventions. Interpreted per the command's own ambiguity rule, and kept in line with the conventions already in use here: this roadmap is the task registry, and the 11 `.claude/agents/NN-*.agent.md` are the routing targets. Reconciling or removing `missao.md` is #59.
- Phase 0 (#38–#40) is deliberately independent of the identity work. It closes two live defects and must not wait for product decisions.
- Existing rows absorbed rather than duplicated: **#30** (hardcoded admins) → #38–#39; **#22** (dead `user-service.ts`) → #51.
- Phase 1 is **blocked** until #40a resolves migration delivery. A login flow depending on tables production lacks is worse than the current mockup. — **Unblocked 2026-11-09**: #40a is done and found no drift, so the path to production is open. The rule it protected still stands, and #41 is deliberately parked at "authored, not applied": no session code lands on top of tables production does not yet have.

## Ingestion Editorial Gate — 2026-11-06

Ingestion publishes directly to the public site. `lib/ingestion/persist.ts:58` writes `status: "PUBLISHED"` on creation, so nothing collected is ever rewritten, rights-checked or reviewed — the site republishes third-party text verbatim under our brand. This is a standing violation of `docs/memory/business-rules.md` § Article lifecycle, which already forbids it.

Target flow: collect → rewrite in NotiLab's voice → validate image rights → human approval → publish. Full spec `docs/editor/ingestion-editorial-workflow.md` (editorial meaning) and `docs/manager/strategy/ingestion-editorial-gate.md` (architecture and phasing).

**Phase 4 is the real project, not Phase 5.** Flipping the gate is one line; building somewhere for the drafts to go is weeks — `components/admin-news-manager.tsx` and `components/workflow-manager.tsx` are shells over `useState` mock arrays with no `fetch`, and `app/api/admin/**` has no lifecycle route at all.
